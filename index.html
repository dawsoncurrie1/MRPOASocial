MRPOA Social Media Strategy

A year-round plan to grow visibility, drive membership, and build community on the Moon River — with pre-written content, automation tools, and zero cost.

Moon River Property Owners' Association · 2025–2026 Season · Prepared for Board Review
The Opportunity
775 followers. 2 talking. We can do better.

MRPOA's Facebook page has a solid follower base but almost zero consistent engagement. Posts happen around events and then go silent for weeks. The algorithm punishes inconsistency — meaning fewer people see each post over time.

The fix isn't more effort. It's a system — pre-written content, scheduled in advance, mixing our own events with community engagement and partner content so the page feels alive year-round. Total time commitment: 30 minutes per month of batch scheduling, plus 15 minutes per event for real-time recaps.

Content Strategy
Four Pillars — Never Just One Note

Every post falls into one of four categories. The mix ensures variety and stops the page from feeling like a one-note bulletin board.

40%
Our Events
Jazz Cruise, Regatta, Fishing Derby, Golf, AGMs. Each gets a 4-post arc: promo, hype, day-of, recap. Real photos of real people.
25%
Community & Engagement
Ice-out predictions, throwback photos, "share your favourite memory," fall colour contests. Posts that make people comment and share.
20%
Education & Stewardship
Water quality data (our own since 2007), Moon River Code, shoreline tips, dark sky, bear safety, septic care. Positions MRPOA as the expert.
15%
Partner & Civic
Township of Muskoka Lakes news + FOCA resources — always remixed with MRPOA's local angle, never just reshared.

The golden rule: Never hit "share" on a Township or FOCA post. Write your own caption with MRPOA's angle. "Here's what this means for Moon River property owners..." turns generic news into content your members actually care about.

How It Gets Done
The 30-Minute Monthly Workflow

Batch Scheduling — Once Per Month

1
Open the 52-Week Calendar spreadsheet (provided). Find this month's 3–5 pre-written posts.
2
Open Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com) — free tool from Facebook. Go to Planner → Create Post.
3
Copy/paste each caption from the spreadsheet. Attach the suggested photo from Google Photos or Canva.
4
Set the date and time (Thurs/Fri 11AM for weekdays, Saturday 9AM for weekends). Click Schedule.
5
Done for the month. For events: assign one person to take photos and post the recap same-day (the only post that can't be pre-scheduled).
